## Frozen delivery policy

The current frozen shipping and delivery specification is maintained in [`SHIPPING_POLICY.md`](./SHIPPING_POLICY.md).

Key locked boundaries:
- EC direct shipping: free from ¥150,000
- Physical-store delivery: free within 1 km from the first purchase source or from ¥250,000
- Store meeting: free
- Calculated customer shipping/delivery under ¥500: ¥0
- Standard self-handling guide: up to 100 cm total dimensions, 10 kg, 1 package
- Large/heavy items: retailer official delivery may be used; paid retailer delivery/installation/carry-in costs are passed through to the customer and confirmed at quote time

Launch/checkout flags remain disabled while staging verification is in progress.
